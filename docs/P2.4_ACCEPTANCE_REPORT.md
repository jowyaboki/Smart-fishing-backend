### 1. Executive Summary

Milestone P2.4 has successfully implemented, audited, refined, software-tested, and integrated the **Empirical Tide Validation Engine**. This engine pairs stored hydrographic sea-level observations with astronomical harmonic predictions from `MoroccanHarmonicTideProvider`, computes rigorous scalar and extrema error metrics, classifies data suitability against oceanographic standards (including strict 85.0% discrete hourly slot completeness, mandatory provenance unit metadata, mathematical conversion consistency across finite numbers with `Number.isFinite()`, single-assignment exclusion without double-counting, and requiring `INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED` datum with full traceable benchmark audit proof for `SUITABLE`), and records validation runs in SQLite WAL storage exposed via REST endpoints.
